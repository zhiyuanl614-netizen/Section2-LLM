Clean energy and water are two crucial resources that must be delivered safely in every civilization. 
Water distribution and electricity transmission networks, which provide these precious resources, have 
traditionally been conceived of as independent, uncoupled systems. However, they are inextricably linked 
in what is known as the energy-water nexus in reality. This paper considers a nexus system constructed 
from combined water-power (CWP) and single water and power energy resources to satisfy the power 
and water demands. Furthermore, a robust optimization technique (ROT) is proposed to model the un- 
certainties of water and power demands. ROT models the worst realizations of the uncertain parameters. 
The obtained results are proper strategy for the conservative system operators. The robust-based opti- 
mization problem is solved using OQNLP solver under the GAMS software. Results state that by consid- 
ering the proposed robust-based optimization model, the total operation cost will be increased by about 
9.52% for the conservative operators.

1. Introduction 
1.1. motivation 
Recently, lots of studies came to attention in the combined 
energy resources section (Fanwei Meng et al., 2018 ). As a result, 
strict and helpful policies have been adopted in several nations 
( Peng et al., 2021 ). This combined energy nexus system involves 
two energy resources like water and power energies ( Wang et al., 
2019 ). Nexus system has some prerequisites for every speciﬁc 
country, which differs from city to city according to the infrastruc- 
ture and geographical locations. Using this technology, particularly 
in the famine and drought regions, will be a promising way to ful- 
ﬁll the water and power demands ( Duan et al., 2021 ). Energy re- 
sources consumption soaring, various policies of nations, and as a 
more critical factor world population ascending, made the politi- 
cians come up with a solution to meet the food, water, and power 
demands ( Kermani et al., 2017 ). On the other hand, the lack of wa- 
ter resources made a culprit issue for electric energy generation.

1.2. Literature review 
In ( Chen et al., 2019 ), inter-regional trade is proposed for a city- 
scale framework to investigate direct and embodied energies such 
as water and power resources. A review paper researched the en- 
ergy, water, and carbon dioxide nexus system and investigated it 
from every aspect. Finally, it introduced hints for the nexus system 
(Fanxin Meng et al., 2019 ). Possible ways of managing the nexus 
system through three integrated management strategies (INRM, 
IWRM, ISWM) are investigated in ( Roidt & Avellán, 2019 ). A de- 
tailed understanding of the analysis of the nexus system is pro- 
posed, and the main problems are investigated to obtain a bet- 
ter result from these systems in ( Cabello et al., 2019 ). Accord- 
ing to ( Namany et al., 2019b ), a multi-objective stochastic opti- 
mization approach is used to realize the optimum way of satis- 
fying water and power energies and obtain a sustainable system in 
the trade-off market. In ( Guan et al., 2019 ), systematic research is 
adopted to analyze the water and power demand energy resources 
by considering land resources as a national project in China. To 
achieve an inter-connected and robust approach in the nexus sys- 
tem and considering agricultural aspects, computational scaffold- 
ing of a framework is proposed in ( Govindan & Al-Ansari, 2019 ). 
In ( Namany et al., 2019a ), agent-based modeling and game the- 
ory methods are used for dynamic decision-making to attract the 
attention of policymakers. In ( Xu et al., 2019 ), different types of 
intertwined virtual water and power resources are investigated to 
fulﬁll the missing knowledge by considering China as a case study. 
In ( Tsolas et al., 2019 ), an organized approach is proposed to design 
and optimize the nexus network using a graph-theoretic procedure 
under the two different cases of minimum and maximum genera- 
tion of both water and power energies. In ( Biggs et al., 2015 ), the 
dependence of human life and nexus approaches and its relation 
with the world’s population rise is analyzed. An integrated method 
that identiﬁes the intertwined nexus systems with the food section 
is investigated on a household scale, and as a result, parameters 
such as family size, amount of income, the diet had a signiﬁcant 
impact on these systems ( Hussien et al., 2017 ). In ( Obringer et al., 
2019 ), a multi-response framework is proposed to the water-power 
demand nexus system by considering climate change. 
In ( Jabari et al., 2019 ), a day-ahead optimization procedure was 
applied to a water-heat-power system by considering all techni- 
cal constraints to minimize the fuel cost of the water desalina- 
tion unit. A comprehensive review of desalination units integrated 
with renewable energy resources alongside essential factors such 
as base technology, cost of operation, and environmental issues 
are investigated ( Alkaisi et al., 2017 ). In ( Hicks et al., 1989 ), all the 
required principles of designing, operation, and citing of desalina- 
tion units are reviewed. In ( Shahzad et al., 2017 ), a state of art re- 
view under the intertwined energy resources is expressed to save 
energy and investigate environmental issues. Cogeneration plants, 
which produce both water and electricity at the same time, are 
common in the Gulf Region. They’ve been shown to be more ther- 
modynamically successful and economically viable than only one 
power and water facilities ( Hamed et al., 2006 ). A water-power 
nexus system combined with air-heated humidiﬁcation dehumidi- 
ﬁcation (HDH) desalination unit is proposed in ( He et al., 2017 ), to 
satisfy the water and power demands. In ( He et al., 2018 ), for spe- 
ciﬁc values of water production and gained-output ratio, a system 
is introduced to take advantage of the cost for water production 
from the desalination unit. In ( Arani et al., 2017 ), a strategy for an- 
alyzing the economic issues of thermal systems is proposed; other 
units such as combined gas turbine and desalination units are in- 
vestigated. Intertwined water and power energy resources are in- 
vestigated for a city through a system-based framework in ( Chen 
& Chen, 2016 ). In the stated research, consumed energy and water 
consumption are merged into uniﬁed hybrid energy. These units 
are compared separately to obtain effective results. 
The water and power uncertainties have signiﬁcant effects on 
the optimal operation of the nexus system. The impacts of the wa- 
ter uncertainty are investigated in ( Guieysse et al., 2013 ). The wa-

1.3. Novelty and contribution 
Based on the reviewed papers, the modeling of the uncertainty 
in the water and power nexus system has not been investigated 
decently. Moreover, according to the reviewed papers, the water 
and power demand’s uncertainties have considerable impacts on 
the nexus systems. Thus, in this paper, the power and water de- 
mand’s uncertainty is considered a big challenge of the nexus sys- 
tems. Modeling the uncertainties using robus optimization is the 
contribution of this paper. To deal with the uncertainty of nexus 
systems, which has a great impact on the water and power de- 
mands, using one of the optimization approaches will be a promis- 
ing way to handle the uncertainty of these systems. In this paper, 
the robust optimization technique (ROT) will be used for optimiz- 
ing the water and power demands for the proposed nexus system. 
Also, a deterministic approach will be applied to the nexus system 
to point out the difference between the ROT and deterministic ap- 
proaches. So, the arrangement of the proposed paper will be as 
follows: 
• Applying the robust optimization technique to model the uncer- 
tainties of the water and power demands in the nexus system 
• Proposing a robust strategy versus deterministic strategy 
Furthermore, the conceptual illustration of the proposed system 
is represented in Fig. 1 .

3. ROT based problem formulation 
The robust optimization approach is a well-known uncer- 
tainty modeling approach in optimization problems. Unlike the 
stochastic-based methods, implementing this approach doesn’t 
need historical data from uncertain parameters, which is advan- 
tageous for the ROT. Hence, this approach can be modeled in the 
optimization problems by deﬁning a deviation range for the un- 
certain parameters. Thus, considering a proper deviation bound for 
the uncertain parameter would be a disadvantage for the ROT. Ac- 
cordingly, the results of the robust optimization give the conser- 
vation results against the deviation of the uncertain parameters.

5. Conclusion 
This study deals with the uncertainty of water and power de- 
mand in a nexus system. This uncertainty is met by taking ad- 
vantage of the optimization approach, ROT. The proposed prob- 
lem is solved through two cases, which were deterministic and the 
ROT approaches. As a result, there is 9.52% increment in the total 
operation robust cost due to implementing the ROT approach by 
considering the following values; for Gama = 0 which stands for 
deterministic approach, obtained total operation robust cost is $ 
8,118.8 and for Gama = 1 which refers to the ROT approach, the 
total correspondent operation robust cost is $ 8,891.9. Due to the 
peak periods and conditions of the producing plants, main pro- 
duced power, water, or cogeneration plants were taking place to 
satisfy the power and water demands in both deterministic and 
ROT cases. As a result, all the water and power demands have been 
satisﬁed through water and power-producing units.
